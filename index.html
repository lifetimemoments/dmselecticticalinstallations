06 QUESTIONS

Fair questions, straight answers.

Do you do free estimates?

Yes, every quote is free. Drop us a line and we'll get back to you quickly with an estimate for the work you need.

Do you carry out repairs?

We undertake all electrical works, whether it's an upgrade to your system or something that simply needs replacing, we cover everything.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Lancing, West Sussex and cover Worthing and the whole of the south-east of England.

Do I really need an electrician?

Electricity can be extremely dangerous. Under no circumstances should you troubleshoot an electrical fault yourself. Call a qualified electrician, for your safety and for the continued safe use of your home's system.
